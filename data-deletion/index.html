Privacy Policy Data Deletion

How to request deletion

If you are a customer or an authorized administrator of a Facebook Page connected to AI Ops Social, you may request deletion of information associated with that connection.

  1. Email adrien01.potier@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line AI Ops Social data deletion request.
  3. Include the name of the business, the Facebook Page name or URL, and a contact address for the response.
  4. Do not include a password, access token, app secret, or other authentication secret.

Verification and completion

AI Ops Social may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify that the requester is authorized to act for the relevant business or Facebook Page. After verification, information within AI Ops Social’s control that is associated with the request will be deleted unless retention is required by applicable law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

The requester will receive a response confirming completion or explaining what additional information is needed.

Disconnecting Meta access

You may also revoke AI Ops Social’s access through the settings provided by Meta. Revoking access stops future access through that authorization, but it may not by itself delete information previously processed by AI Ops Social. Use the email procedure above to request deletion from AI Ops Social.

Security reminder: Never send passwords, Meta access tokens, app secrets, or other credentials in a deletion request.

More information

See the AI Ops Social Privacy Policy for additional information about the categories of data processed and the purposes of processing.
