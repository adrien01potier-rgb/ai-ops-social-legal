Privacy Policy Data Deletion

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Ops Social processes information when a business customer voluntarily connects one or more Facebook Pages to the AI Ops Social service. It applies to the AI Ops Social Meta application and the workflows the customer authorizes through that application.

AI Ops Social is intended for business customers and their authorized personnel. It is not intended for personal or consumer use.

2. Information processed through Meta

Depending on the permissions approved by Meta and granted by the customer, AI Ops Social may process:

  • Facebook Page identifiers, Page names, and the list of Pages the connecting person is permitted to manage;
  • the authorization information necessary to maintain the customer-approved connection to Meta;
  • Page content that the customer asks AI Ops Social to create, publish, schedule, update, or manage;
  • Page engagement information and performance insights made available by Meta for the authorized Pages; and
  • technical and operational records needed to execute and troubleshoot the customer’s requested workflows.

AI Ops Social does not use these permissions to access Pages that the customer has not selected or is not authorized to manage.

3. Meta permissions and purposes

The application may request the following permissions, subject to Meta approval and the customer’s authorization:

  • pages_show_list — to identify the Facebook Pages the customer is authorized to connect;
  • pages_manage_posts — to publish and manage Page content at the customer’s instruction;
  • pages_read_engagement — to read Page content and engagement information for reporting and workflow operation; and
  • read_insights — to retrieve Page performance metrics and prepare analytics for the customer.

Information received through these permissions is processed only to provide the connected-Page management, publishing, automation, reporting, and performance-analysis functions requested by the customer.

4. Customer choice and authorization

A business customer chooses whether to connect Facebook Pages and which available Pages to authorize. The customer may revoke the connection through Meta’s settings and may also ask AI Ops Social to disconnect a Page or delete associated information.

5. Storage and retention

Data handling depends on the workflow and deployment configured for the customer. AI Ops Social does not state a universal fixed retention period where none has been established for that configuration.

Meta-derived information and authorization data may be retained only to the extent needed to operate the customer-authorized workflow, maintain security, troubleshoot the service, comply with applicable requirements, and complete a verified deletion request. When information is no longer needed for those purposes, it will be deleted or de-identified as appropriate.

Customers may request details about the storage and retention settings applicable to their own deployment by using the contact information below.

6. Disclosure and service operation

AI Ops Social may make information available to the customer that authorized the connection and to people acting on that customer’s behalf. Information may also be processed by technical service providers only where needed to operate, secure, or support the service, or disclosed where required by law. AI Ops Social does not grant another business independent rights to use Meta-derived information for its own unrelated purposes.

7. Security

AI Ops Social applies reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information processed and restricts access to people and systems that need it to operate the authorized service. No method of electronic processing is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data deletion

A customer or authorized user may request deletion of information associated with a connected Facebook Page by following the instructions on the Data Deletion page or by emailing adrien01.potier@gmail.com with the subject “AI Ops Social data deletion request.”

To protect customers and Page administrators, AI Ops Social may request information reasonably necessary to verify the requester’s authority. After verification, information within AI Ops Social’s control that is associated with the request will be deleted unless retention is required by applicable law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. The requester will be informed when the request has been completed or if additional information is required.

9. Privacy requests and questions

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions relating to AI Ops Social, email adrien01.potier@gmail.com. Please identify the business and Facebook Page concerned, but do not send passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service, requested permissions, data practices, or legal requirements change. The “last updated” date above will be revised when changes are published. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate customer channel where required.
